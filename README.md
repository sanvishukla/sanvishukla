# 💫 About Me:

Hi, I’m **Sanvi!**  
I’m a final-year Computer Science undergraduate who enjoys building ML systems that work on real data and real constraints.

Most of my work sits at the intersection of **machine learning, decision systems, and real-world deployment**. I’ve worked as a research intern at the **University of New Brunswick (Mitacs)** and **IIT Gandhinagar**, where I focused on combining **transformers, reinforcement learning, and practical pipelines**. One of my projects on accessibility in mobile systems was published at **India HCI 2025**.

---

### 💡 What I Enjoy Working On

- Machine Learning systems that go beyond offline evaluation  
- AI for real-world and high-impact use cases  
- End-to-end systems (model → pipeline → deployment)  
- Full-stack + mobile apps that integrate ML meaningfully
